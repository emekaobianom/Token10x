Account Deletion Request

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated personal data from Token10x Market: Crypto Tracker.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you wish to permanently delete your Token10x Market account, send an email to our support team using the address below.

Please include the email address associated with your Token10x account so that we can identify and verify your account.

Request Account Deletion

Alternatively, send your request directly to: contact@token10x.com

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted?

Once your account deletion request has been verified and processed, the following account-associated information will be deleted:

  • Your Token10x user account and profile information.
  • Your registered email address and associated account details.
  • Your saved cryptocurrency watchlists.
  • Your virtual portfolio and virtual trading positions.
  • Your virtual trading history and associated practice data.

Data Retention

We do not retain personal account data after a completed deletion request, except where retention is necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or protect against fraud and abuse.

Any information retained for these purposes will be limited to what is necessary and securely stored. It will be deleted when the applicable retention requirement or purpose ends.

Processing Time

We will review your request, verify account ownership where necessary, and process eligible deletion requests within 30 days of receiving the required information.
